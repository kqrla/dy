# overview

the dysfunctional executives is a personal knowledge system for people who think across disciplines. it covers electronics, thinking methods, and the tools to capture and connect ideas.

the site is organized around four areas:

**thinking better** covers concepts like polymath, multipotentialite, and interdisciplinary thinking. these are the ideas that explain why breadth matters and how cross-domain knowledge creates insight that specialists miss.

**memory** covers how you retain, recall, and organize what you know. retentive memory, knowledge networks, second brain, and digital gardens are the frameworks for making your knowledge durable and connected.

**knowledge management** is the practical layer: tools for thinking, techniques for learning, graphic organizers for structuring thought, and sketchnotes for visual capture.

**electronics** is the applied domain. circuit symbols, power, resistors, capacitors, diodes, transistors, timers, and microcontrollers. these pages teach the fundamentals of building things with electronics.

the site is built as a static astro application. every page is pre-built html. there is no database, no user accounts, and no server-side logic. the theme system supports dark and light modes with four background patterns. all icons use font awesome for consistent rendering across operating systems.

the design is intentional: monospace type, pastel accents, hand-drawn highlights, and a notebook-like feel. the site should feel like a well-kept reference notebook, not a corporate wiki.
